    Digital services overview

    An overview of digital services that researchers at KTH can use for storing, computing on, sharing and publishing research material. Search freely, filter by provider, function group or service type, or click any tag to see everything that matches it. Select a service to open its card with access details, guides and further information.

    Missing information is marked as uppgift saknas rather than left out. Costs are described in relation to other options instead of as a price list, and risks are described the same way for all providers.

    Zenodo

    CERN / EU

    General-purpose repository for publishing datasets, software and other research output with a DOI.

    Overall Risk Profile: Risks or conditions identified
    Legal & GDPRRisks or conditions identified
    Intellectual Property (IP)Under review / partial assessment
    Information SecurityUnder review / partial assessment
    Cost ModelNo significant known risks
    Support & AvailabilityNo significant known risks
    Access

    No agreement or application needed. Uploads up to 50 GB per record are free; larger datasets can be requested.

    Guides
    1. Sign in at https://zenodo.org with ORCID so your output is linked to you.
    2. Choose New upload, add files and fill in title, authors and licence.
    3. Reserve a DOI if you need to cite the dataset before publishing.
    4. Publish — the record and DOI become permanent.
    About the service

    Zenodo supports FAIR publishing: persistent identifiers, open licences, versioning and machine-readable metadata. Records can be embargoed or restricted, but Zenodo is not a service for personal or confidential data.

    EOSC EU Node storage

    EOSC

    European Open Science Cloud storage and compute for project data, including Jupyter notebooks close to the data.

    Overall Risk Profile: Risks or conditions identified
    Legal & GDPRRisks or conditions identified
    Intellectual Property (IP)Assessment unavailable
    Information SecurityAssessment unavailable
    Cost ModelNo significant known risks
    Support & AvailabilityRisks or conditions identified
    Access

    Sign in with your institutional identity (KTH) via the EOSC EU Node portal. A baseline allocation is free; larger allocations are requested through the portal — uppgift saknas about current quota limits.

    Guides
    1. Open the EOSC EU Node portal and sign in with your KTH account.
    2. Choose File sync and share for storage, or Interactive notebooks for Jupyter.

    About the service

    Data stays within EU infrastructure, which helps with funder and regulatory requirements on location. The service is developing quickly, so availability and quotas may change over the lifetime of a project.

    Sharing Data with External Partners

    KTH Library

    A practical guide on how to securely collaborate and share research data with partners outside KTH.

    Principles & Guidelines

    Before sharing data externally, ensure compliance with confidentiality requirements, GDPR, and funder grant agreements.

    Step by Step
    1. Identify data classification: Does the dataset contain personal data, export-controlled information, or commercial trade secrets?
    2. Select an approved repository or workspace: Use managed environments (such as KTH OneDrive or approved institutional cloud nodes).
    3. Set role-based permissions: Grant access strictly to named individuals rather than generating open public share links.
    Legal & Transfer Agreements

    When sharing confidential or sensitive data across organizations, a Data Transfer Agreement (DTA) is often required. Consult KTH Legal Counsel when in doubt.

    GitHub

    Microsoft

    Proprietary code forge and developer platform that allows anyone to create an account and store, manage, and share their code.

    Overall Risk Profile: Risks or conditions identified
    Legal & GDPRRisks or conditions identified

    Suitable for source code and collaborative development. Sensitive personal information and regulated data require separate assessment.

    Intellectual Property (IP)Risks or conditions identified

    Users should understand licence terms, ownership arrangements, and the rights associated with external contributions.

    Information SecurityNo significant known risks

    Supports multi-factor authentication and institutional identity integration.

    Cost ModelNo significant known risks
    Support & AvailabilityRisks or conditions identified
    Access

    Create a personal account and ask the relevant KTH organisation owner to add you. Private repositories are included at no cost for academic use.

    Guides
    1. Create a repository and add a README.md and a licence file.
    2. Clone it locally with git clone and commit your work regularly.
    3. Use branches and pull requests when several people work in parallel.
    4. Connect the repository to Zenodo to get a DOI for each release.
    About the service

    GitHub is well suited for code and text-based material. It should not be used for personal data or large binary datasets. Data stored on GitHub is hosted globally, which must be considered when the project has location requirements.

    Checklist for Dataset Publication

    Research Data Office (RDO)

    Verify that your dataset complies with the FAIR principles and KTH institutional policies prior to open release.

    Overall Risk Profile: No significant known risks
    Legal & GDPRNo significant known risks
    Intellectual Property (IP)No significant known risks
    Information SecurityNo significant known risks
    Cost ModelNo significant known risks
    Support & AvailabilityNo significant known risks
    Pre-Deposit Checks
    • [ ] Is a comprehensive README.txt or README.md included in the root folder?
    • [ ] Have all personal identifiers been scrubbed, pseudonymized, or formally cleared?
    • [ ] Are datasets formatted in open, non-proprietary standards (e.g., CSV, JSON, NetCDF, PDF/A)?
    Documentation & Licensing
    • [ ] Has an open license been designated (e.g., CC0 or CC BY 4.0)?
    • [ ] Are all creators and contributors registered with their ORCID identifiers?
    Post-Deposit Actions
    • [ ] Register the issued DOI in DiVA.
    • [ ] Cross-link the dataset DOI within the corresponding peer-reviewed publication.

    Note that it is impossible to use the software without an internet connection! From their privacy policy:

    in order to use the software, you must activate it with your serial number. [...] an Internet connection is mandatory for activating the software

    Note that if you use their "TeamCloud" add-on module your data will be shared via their cloud. According to their FAQ, TeamCloud stores user data on GDPR-compliant AWS servers in Germany.

    The company is based in the European Union, which should mean we can expect GDPR compliance by the company.

    From Wikipedia:

    Its functions include organizing and coding source materials, writing memos, retrieving and querying coded segments, managing cases and associated variables, and producing tables and visualizations.
