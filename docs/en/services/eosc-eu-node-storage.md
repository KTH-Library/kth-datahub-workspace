## Access

Sign in with your institutional identity through the platform portal. A
baseline allocation is available without charge. Larger allocations can be
requested through the portal. Information about current quota limits is not
available.

## Guides

1. Open the platform portal and sign in with your institutional account.
2. Choose **File Storage** for data storage or **Interactive Notebooks** for analysis.
3. Upload or connect your project data.
4. Share project resources with collaborators using their platform identities.

## About the Service

Data is hosted within European infrastructure, which may support compliance
with funding and regulatory requirements related to data location. The service
is evolving continuously, and availability, features, and quota limits may
change over time.
