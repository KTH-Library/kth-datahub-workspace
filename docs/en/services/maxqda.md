## Access

KTH does **not** offer a license nor support for MaxQDA.
To buy a license for MaxQDA or its add-ons you must pay their annual subscription.

## About the software

Although the software runs locally on your computer, the license is non-free and
the source code is not inspectable, thus it is impossible to say what data,
if any, leaves your device.

Note that it is impossible to use the software without an internet connection!
From their [privacy policy](https://www.maxqda.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/VERBI_Privacy_Policy_Software_ENG.pdf):

> in order to use the software, you must activate it with your serial number.
> [...] an Internet connection is mandatory for activating the software

Note that if you use their ["TeamCloud" add-on module](https://www.maxqda.com/teamcloud)
your data *will* be shared via their cloud.
[According to their FAQ](https://help.maxqda.com/en/support/solutions/articles/80001135559-where-is-the-data-stored-when-using-maxqda-teamcloud-is-data-protection-guaranteed-),
TeamCloud stores user data on GDPR-compliant AWS servers in Germany.

The company is based in the European Union, which should mean we can
expect GDPR compliance by the company.

From [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAXQDA):

> Its functions include organizing and coding source materials, writing memos,
> retrieving and querying coded segments, managing cases and associated variables,
> and producing tables and visualizations.
