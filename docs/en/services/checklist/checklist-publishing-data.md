## Pre-Deposit Checks
- [ ] Is a comprehensive `README.txt` or `README.md` included in the root folder?
- [ ] Have all personal identifiers been scrubbed, pseudonymized, or formally cleared?
- [ ] Are datasets formatted in open, non-proprietary standards (e.g., CSV, JSON, NetCDF, PDF/A)?

## Documentation & Licensing
- [ ] Has an open license been designated (e.g., CC0 or CC BY 4.0)?
- [ ] Are all creators and contributors registered with their ORCID identifiers?

## Post-Deposit Actions
- [ ] Register the issued DOI in DiVA.
- [ ] Cross-link the dataset DOI within the corresponding peer-reviewed publication.
