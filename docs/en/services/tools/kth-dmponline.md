## Access

Create an account to access KTH's DMP templates.
Once you have an account you can link this to your university account and log in
with your KTH credentials.

The information you enter in KTH DMPonline will **not** become available to the public
unless you choose to make it so. However, KTH Research Data Services can access
the information. This information may be used for support purposes, but also for reuse
in administrative systems to simplify and provide better services.


## Guides

Do not enter information about high-security projects into KTH DMPonline.
If you need a DMP for such a project, feel free to log in, create a test plan and
*download* the questions from the appropriate template. Use your template and fill
in your answers in order to write a DMP using your preferred *local* writing program
and store the DMP in a secure location.


## About the service

This instance of DMPonline is provided by KTH to help you write data management plans.
