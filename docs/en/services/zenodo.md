## Access

No agreement or application is required. Standard deposits are available at no
cost. Information about maximum dataset size and project allocations may vary
and should be verified with the service provider.

## Guides

1. Sign in using a researcher, institutional, or project account.
2. Create a new record and upload files.
3. Provide metadata such as title, authors, keywords, and licence.
4. Reserve a persistent identifier if early citation is required.
5. Publish the record when the dataset or software is ready for dissemination.

## About the Service

The service supports FAIR publication practices by providing persistent
identifiers, versioning, machine-readable metadata, and support for open
licensing. Records may be published immediately, embargoed for a defined
period, or shared with restricted access depending on project requirements.
The platform is intended for published research outputs rather than
confidential or sensitive information.
