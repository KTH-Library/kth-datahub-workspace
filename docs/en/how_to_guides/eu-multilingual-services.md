# Get started with Digital Europe's Multilingual Services

The European Commission offers a free collection of [multilingual translation
and language tools](https://translation.ec.europa.eu/tools-and-resources/ai-translation-and-language-tools_en)
that support all 24 EU languages plus a few more.

Explore the full range of tools on [the Language Tools portal](https://language-tools.ec.europa.eu).

The eTranslation tool is perhaps the most useful for researchers, but there are
several others, including a Speech-to-Text service.
Visit their very useful [help pages for video guides, step-by-step instructions and tool-specific support](https://language-tools.ec.europa.eu/help?lang=en&app=videos).

To use the eTranslation service you need to

1. [create an "EU login" account](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/cas/eim/external/register.cgi), and
2. [register your EU login account](https://language-tools.ec.europa.eu/welcome) on the eTranslate service.

If you have done both steps, you should then be able to use the eTranslate service at  
<https://language-tools.ec.europa.eu/ETranslationServices>.

Note: once logged in, you are served the "Translate documents" view by default,
which works for uploaded documents. But there is also a "Translate text" view
(see the blue menu bar) that works for free-text.

These services are developed through the Digital Europe programme
by the Directorate-General for Translation of the European Commission.

It says that APIs are available integration into your own systems, but
I could not find any more documentation regarding that.

Note that there is a usage limit, albeit high:

> Individual users of eTranslation are limited to 100 document translations per day.
> A single document into four languages counts as four translations.  
> <https://language-tools.ec.europa.eu/help?lang=en&app=etr>
