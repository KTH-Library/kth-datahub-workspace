## Access

No agreement or application needed. Uploads up to 50 GB per record are free;
larger datasets can be requested.

## Guides

1. Sign in at <https://zenodo.org> with ORCID so your output is linked to you.
2. Choose **New upload**, add files and fill in title, authors and licence.
3. Reserve a DOI if you need to cite the dataset before publishing.
4. Publish — the record and DOI become permanent.

## About the service

Zenodo supports FAIR publishing: persistent identifiers, open licences,
versioning and machine-readable metadata. Records can be embargoed or
restricted, but Zenodo is not a service for personal or confidential data.
