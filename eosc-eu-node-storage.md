## Access

Sign in with your institutional identity (KTH) via the EOSC EU Node portal. A
baseline allocation is free; larger allocations are requested through the
portal — uppgift saknas about current quota limits.

## Guides

1. Open the EOSC EU Node portal and sign in with your KTH account.
2. Choose **File sync and share** for storage, or **Interactive notebooks** for Jupyter.
3. Upload or mount your project data.
4. Share folders with project members using their EOSC identities.

## About the service

Data stays within EU infrastructure, which helps with funder and regulatory
requirements on location. The service is developing quickly, so availability
and quotas may change over the lifetime of a project.
